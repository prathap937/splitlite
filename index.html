Sync with another device

Peer-to-peer over WebRTC — no server or account involved. Both sides exchange one short code manually (paste via chat, email, etc.), then your data transfers directly browser-to-browser. This works best when both people can reach the internet normally (no VPN/strict firewall); a corporate network can sometimes block the direct connection.
